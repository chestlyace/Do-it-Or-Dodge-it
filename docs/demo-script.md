# Demo script (1 to 2 minutes)

Record the phone's screen with Android's built-in screen recorder, in portrait,
with the phone in airplane mode for the whole take (the status bar shows it).
Do the model download beforehand; the demo is about using the app.

Before you start: the model is installed and there are no tasks. Turn on airplane
mode last.

A nudge needs at least a minute to arrive, even with the fast clock (long-press
the Home title, bell icon, **Fast clock**). So record that part as a separate
clip: start **Do It** on a task, leave the app, wait for the nudge, tap it. Cut
that clip in at 0:52.

| Time | Show | Say (or put on screen) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | The Home screen, empty. Airplane mode visible in the status bar | "This app runs a Gemma model on my phone. No internet, no account." |
| 0:08 | Tap **Add task**, type "Do my tax return", pick Admin, add a due date, **Add task** | "Here is the task I keep avoiding." |
| 0:18 | Tap the task. Point at **Dodge risk** and its reason | "It has learned from my history how likely I am to dodge this, and why." |
| 0:26 | Tap **Do It**. Steps stream in one at a time | "Gemma breaks it into steps so small they are hard to refuse. The first takes under two minutes." |
| 0:40 | Tick the first step. Tap **Help me** on a step and show the draft, then **Copy draft** | "It can draft the email or message for a step too." |
| 0:52 | The nudge clip: a notification arrives on the lock screen or shade; tap it and the task opens | "Then it nudges me, slowly and steadily, about only the next step." |
| 1:05 | Back on Home, add "Reply to the landlord". Tap **Dodge It** | "Or I can dodge it." |
| 1:12 | Type "landlord", pick **Funny**, **Write my excuse**, **Share** to WhatsApp | "Gemma writes the excuse and I send it myself. The app never sends anything." |
| 1:28 | Open **History** | "The dodge is logged, never hidden. That is what it learns from." |
| 1:38 | Open **Settings**, show quiet hours and the model info | "Quiet hours, limits, and the whole thing runs offline." |
| 1:45 | End on the Home screen | "Do It or Dodge It. Everything stays on your phone." |

Tips:

- Do a dry run first; the first model load takes a few seconds, so open a task
  once beforehand to warm things up (the app loads the model while you look at a
  task).
- To check notifications work before a take, tap **Test notification** on the
  Nudges screen (it arrives in 10 seconds).
- Keep notifications from other apps off so nothing private shows on screen.
